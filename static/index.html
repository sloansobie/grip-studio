How to measure your hand ↗

Keep the hand flat, fingers straight and together, thumb relaxed away. Measure hand length from the wrist crease to the middle fingertip; palm length from that same crease to the base crease of the middle finger. Breadth is across the knuckles, excluding the thumb.

Use these same landmarks for every person. They define this app’s measurement protocol; do not mix measurements from different ANSUR landmark definitions.
